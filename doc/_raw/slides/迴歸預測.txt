The Role of Forecasting
Forecasting is a vital function and affects every 
significant management decision.
Finance and accounting use forecasts as the basis 
for budgeting and cost control.
Sales and marketing relies on forecasts to make key 
decisions such as new product planning and 
personnel compensation.
Production uses forecasts to select suppliers; 
determine capacity requirements; and drive 
decisions about purchasing, staffing, and inventory.

Simple Moving Average
Forecast is the average of a fixed number of past 
periods (以過去歷史資料的平均預測未來).
Useful when demand is not growing or declining 
rapidly and no seasonality is present.
Removes some of the random fluctuation from the 
data.
Selecting the period length is important.
– Longer periods provide more smoothing.
– Shorter periods react to trends more quickly.

Weighted Moving Average
The simple moving average formula implies equal 
weighting for all periods (簡單移動平均假設不同時
間的資料權重均相同).
A weighted moving average allows unequal 
weighting of prior time periods.
– The sum of the weights must be equal to one.
– Often, more recent periods are given higher 
weights than periods farther in the past.
– 加權移動平均的問題在於不知如何決定不同時
間點的資料權重

Exponential Smoothing
A weighted average method that includes all past data in 
the forecasting calculation
More recent results weighted more heavily
The most used of all forecasting techniques
An integral part of computerized forecasting
Well accepted for six reasons
Exponential models are surprisingly accurate.
Formulating an exponential model is relatively easy.
Little computation is required to use the model.
Computer storage requirements are small.
Tests for accuracy are easy to compute.

Criteria for Selecting Methods

Criteria to use in making forecast method and parameter 
choices include 
1. Minimizing MAPE, MAD, or MSE
2. Meeting managerial expectations of changes in the 
components of demand
3. Minimizing the forecast error last period
Statistical performance measures can be used
1. For projections of more stable demand patterns, use 
lower α values or larger n values
2. For projections of more dynamic demand patterns try 
higher α values or smaller n values

MARS介紹
統計物理學家Friedman 於1991 年提出多元適應雲形
迴歸，其屬於一種多變量無母數迴歸處理的程序。
其能夠正確的預測連續與二元的相依變數，找出隱
藏在高維度資料中的複雜結構及變數的交互作用。
過去對於資料之分析，常常不論資料是否符合常態
，均用線性迴歸進行實證，導致模型適用性出現問
題，而MARS為多變量無母數分析技術，相較於傳統
之線性迴歸，可解決資料非線性、非常態等問題。

(MARS多元自適雲形迴歸)

脊迴歸(Ridge Regression)
進行迴歸分析時，當遭遇以下幾種情況時，可能要注意迴歸
模式中是否存在多元共線性(multi-collinearity)的問題：
共線性指標超過標準
迴歸係數的方向性與相關係數相反
解釋力R平方很高，但個別變項的係數未達顯著水準
解決共線性的方法如下：
刪除共線性X變項
利用主成分分析將線性重合的自變數重新建構成新的潛
在變項，來替代原有的自變數
使用「偏差迴歸分析法」(Biased Regression Analysis,簡
稱BRA)，如使用Ridge regression analysis (脊迴歸)。

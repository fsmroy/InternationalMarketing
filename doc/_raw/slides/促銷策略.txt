Association Rule Mining Task
Given a set of transactions T, the goal of 
association rule mining is to find all rules having 
support ≥ minsup threshold
confidence ≥ minconf threshold
Brute-force approach:
List all possible association rules
Compute the support and confidence for each rule
Prune rules that fail the minsup and minconf
thresholds
Computationally prohibitive!

Statistical Independence
Population of 1000 students
600 students know how to swim (S)
700 students know how to bike (B)
420 students know how to swim and bike (S,B)
P(SB) = 420/1000 = 0.42
P(S) P(B) = 0.6 0.7 = 0.42
P(SB) = P(S) P(B) => Statistically independent
P(SB) > P(S) P(B) => Positively correlated
P(SB) < P(S) P(B) => Negatively correlated

Simple Moving Average
Forecast is the average of a fixed number of past 
periods (以過去歷史資料的平均預測未來).
Useful when demand is not growing or declining 
rapidly and no seasonality is present.
Removes some of the random fluctuation from 
the data.
Selecting the period length is important.
Longer periods provide more smoothing.
Shorter periods react to trends more quickly.

Weighted Moving Average
The simple moving average formula implies 
equal weighting for all periods (簡單移動平均
假設不同時間的資料權重均相同).
A weighted moving average allows unequal 
weighting of prior time periods.
The sum of the weights must be equal to one.
Often, more recent periods are given higher weights 
than periods farther in the past.
加權移動平均的問題在於不知如何決定不同時間
點的資料權重

Exponential Smoothing
A weighted average method that includes all past 
data in the forecasting calculation
More recent results weighted more heavily
The most used of all forecasting techniques
An integral part of computerized forecasting
Well accepted for six reasons

Exponential models are surprisingly accurate.

Formulating an exponential model is relatively easy.

Little computation is required to use the model.

Computer storage requirements are small.

Tests for accuracy are easy to compute.

MARS介紹
統計物理學家Friedman 於1991 年提出多元適應雲形
迴歸，其屬於一種多變量無母數迴歸處理的程序。
其能夠正確的預測連續與二元的相依變數，找出隱
藏在高維度資料中的複雜結構及變數的交互作用。
過去對於資料之分析，常常不論資料是否符合常態
，均用線性迴歸進行實證，導致模型適用性出現問
題，而MARS為多變量無母數分析技術，相較於傳統
之線性迴歸，可解決資料非線性、非常態等問題。

傾銷的類別
掠奪性傾銷（Predatory Dumping）：指某一
跨國公司以增加國內的生產成本為代價，或
是刻意虧損，來達成在另一個國家低價銷售
以奪取市場占有率的目的。
無心傾銷（Unintentional Dumping）: 由於交
易日、運送日，和到達日之間的時間落差而
產生的價格差異。由於價格隱含著匯率的因
素，當匯率因為時間的關係而改變時，則有
可能造成最終售價低於其生產成本或母國的
當地價格，因此在無意間造成傾銷。

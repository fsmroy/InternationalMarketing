What are new products?
1. Promoting/exporting current products to new markets
2. Radical innovation like using magnetic RAM to 
replace dynamic RAM,
3. Process improvement to increase yield rate or lower 
costs (OLED displays, solar-cells’ efficiencies)
4. Extension of existing products- from digital cameras to
smart cameras (i.e. Wifi),
5. Repositioning old products- changing medical drugs 
(for therapy) into healthcare drugs (for prevention)
6. Product-service system (PSS): car dealers switch to 
rental & maintenance service by using IoT sensors

===== p27 =====
陽明交通大學工業工程與管理學系
Industrial Practices
為製造而設計或為裝配而設計其目的都為了降低生產成本
儘量以最少的零件來設計產品
最底層零件能盡量作為其他零件組裝時的基座
以按扣方式取代螺絲、螺帽等裝配方式
零件之順序必須考量，以避免裝上後必須取下重裝
零件應容易握持與定位
利用工作研究方法決定作業員動作的經濟原則
使用標準化且易得的更換零件
避免過度客製化要求超出需要的公差

## Problem 1 — AHP 二階層評估（三個港口）

Using AHP to conduct a two-level assessment for three harbors in the world, Kaohsiung, Busan, and Tokyo. The criteria includes living cost, natural environment, city tourism, food, transportation, and courtesy. Please draw a plot to visualize the process of assessment. Then, calculate the average weights of the four criteria based on your team members. Third, conduct assessment for three cities with respect to all criteria. Finally, calculate the weighted score for three cities.

**重點：**

- 目標：以 AHP 對三個港口城市（**Kaohsiung 高雄、Busan 釜山、Tokyo 東京**）進行**二階層（two-level）**評估。
- 準則（criteria）：living cost、natural environment、city tourism、food、transportation、courtesy。
- 要求：
  1. 繪圖視覺化整體評估流程（階層圖）。
  2. 依團隊成員的成對比較計算**各準則的平均權重**（原文寫「four criteria」，疑為筆誤，全文列六項準則）。
  3. 就所有準則對三個城市做評估。
  4. 計算三個城市的**加權分數**。

## Problem 2 — 以 TOPSIS 取代二階層之 AHP

Following Problem 1, replace level-two assessment using AHP by using TOPSIS. Based on a five-point Likert scale, PIS and NIS are respectively (5,5,5,5,5,5) and (1,1,1,1,1,1). Reserve the weights of the criteria obtained in Problem 1 and calculate the weighted TOPSIS score to conduct assessment for the three cities. Discuss the differences of results between Problem 1 and Problem 2.

**重點：**

- 承接 Problem 1，將「第二階層」的 AHP 評估改用 **TOPSIS**。
- 採**五點李克特量表**；正理想解 PIS = (5,5,5,5,5,5)，負理想解 NIS = (1,1,1,1,1,1)。
- **沿用 Problem 1 求得的準則權重**，計算加權 TOPSIS 分數，對三城市評估。
- 討論 Problem 1（AHP）與 Problem 2（TOPSIS）結果的差異。

## Problem 3 — Kano 模型評估智慧型手機

Apply Kano model to do evaluation for smartphones considering the features: water proof, optical zoom for cameras, wireless charging, and foldable screen. Derive the delight and the disgust based on the Kano percentages of your team members.

**重點：**

- 以 **Kano 模型**評估智慧型手機的四項功能：
  - water proof（防水）
  - optical zoom for cameras（相機光學變焦）
  - wireless charging（無線充電）
  - foldable screen（摺疊螢幕）
- 依團隊成員的 **Kano 分類百分比**推導 **delight（滿意）** 與 **disgust（不滿意）**。

## Problem 4 — 二元整數規劃（價格基礎顧客分群）

Following Problem 3, apply binary integer programming to conduct price based customer segmentation. Given the base cost for a smart phone is TWD\$10000, the extra costs inclusive for water proof, optical zoom for cameras, wireless charging, and foldable screen are respectively 2500, 3500, 1500, 155000. The goal is to maximize delight or minimize disgust. Derive their optimal product configuration for three segments: high (30000), medium (22000), and low (15000).

**重點：**

- 承接 Problem 3，以**二元整數規劃（BIP）**做**價格基礎**的顧客分群。
- 參數：
  - 手機基礎成本 = **TWD\$10,000**
  - 各功能額外成本（inclusive）：
    - water proof = **2,500**
    - optical zoom for cameras = **3,500**
    - wireless charging = **1,500**
    - foldable screen = **155,000**（原文如此，疑似筆誤，見備註）
- 目標：**最大化 delight** 或 **最小化 disgust**。
- 對三個價格分群（**high = 30,000、medium = 22,000、low = 15,000**）推導最佳產品配置。

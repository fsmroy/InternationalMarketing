## 題目一：市場區隔 — 統計檢定（bank 資料集）

**原文**

> Based on bank dataset, remove the following columns: job, contact, pdays, poutcome, month, and day. Then, conduct market segmentation using statistical tests with regard to the response "y" (participating in promotion plan or not). For categorical features, apply Chi-square tests. For numerical features, please apply F test first to justify equal variance or not (between two groups, participating vs. non-participating) and then apply T tests to justify equal mean or not. Suppose you are marketing manager, indicate that what features are suitable to conduct market segmentation? Then, address managerial insights for the above-mentioned results.

**中文翻譯**

以 bank 資料集為基礎，先移除以下欄位：job（職業）、contact（聯絡方式）、pdays（上次聯絡間隔天數）、poutcome（上次行銷結果）、month（月份）、day（日期）。

接著針對反應變數「y」（是否參與促銷方案）進行統計檢定，以執行市場區隔：

- **類別型特徵**：使用卡方檢定（Chi-square test）
- **數值型特徵**：先用 F 檢定判斷兩組（參與 vs. 未參與）變異數是否相等，再據此選用對應的 T 檢定判斷兩組平均數是否相等

假設你是行銷經理，請指出哪些特徵適合作為市場區隔的依據，並針對上述結果提出管理意涵（managerial insights）。

## 題目二：關聯規則 — 中位數離散化（bank 資料集）

**原文**

> Based on bank dataset, remove the following columns: job, contact, pdays, poutcome, month, and day. For the column "age", discretize the age into three levels (young <= 35, adult 35~55, old > 55). For the remaining numeric features, using "median" to cut the attributes into two levels: low and high, and treat "y" as the RHS. (a) Identify the significant rules with respect to the RHS ("y-yes" or "y-no"). (b) Count the frequency for each feature shown in the LHS and prioritize the features with respect to the RHS ("y-yes" or "y-no"). (c) Specify managerial insights for the results.

**中文翻譯**

以 bank 資料集為基礎，同樣移除欄位：job、contact、pdays、poutcome、month、day。

- **age（年齡）欄位**：離散化為三個等級——young（年輕，≤ 35）、adult（壯年，35～55）、old（年長，> 55）
- **其餘數值型特徵**：以「中位數（median）」切分為兩個等級：low（低）、high（高）
- 將「y」作為規則的右側（RHS）

任務：

1. **(a)** 找出對應 RHS（「y-yes」或「y-no」）的顯著規則
2. **(b)** 統計各特徵出現在規則左側（LHS）的頻率，並分別針對「y-yes」與「y-no」排出特徵優先順序
3. **(c)** 針對結果提出管理意涵

## 題目三：關聯規則 — 醫療洞察（pima 資料集）

**原文**

> Based on pima dataset, remove samples with zeros in the following columns: Glucose, BloodPressure, SkinThickness, Insulin, and BMI. Then, use Q1 and Q3 to cut the attributes into three levels: low, medium, or high. Apply association rule mining to gain medical insights by observing the LHS coupled with the specific "RHS=Yes" or "RHS=No". Use frequency count to indicate the top key indicators with the specific setting, "RHS=Yes" or "RHS=No", respectively (i.e. what features and corresponding levels are most commonly shown in the LHS for conditions, such as "RHS=Yes" or "RHS=No").

**中文翻譯**

以 pima（糖尿病）資料集為基礎，先移除以下欄位中數值為 0 的樣本（視為缺失值）：Glucose（血糖）、BloodPressure（血壓）、SkinThickness（皮膚厚度）、Insulin（胰島素）、BMI（身體質量指數）。

接著以 Q1（第一四分位數）與 Q3（第三四分位數）將各屬性切分為三個等級：low（低）、medium（中）、high（高）。

套用關聯規則探勘（association rule mining），觀察規則左側（LHS）與特定右側（「RHS=Yes」或「RHS=No」）的組合，以獲得醫療洞察。

分別在「RHS=Yes」與「RHS=No」兩種設定下，用頻率統計找出最關鍵的指標（即：哪些特徵與其對應等級最常出現在 LHS 中）。

## 題目四：多維度尺度分析 MDS — 產品定位（智慧型手機）

**原文**

> Regarding smartphone product series (iPhone Pro, iPhone Pro Max, Samsung S series, Samsung A series, Xiaomi, Redmi, Oppo X series, Oppo A series), apply multi-dimensional scaling (MDS) to conduct product positioning. Considering the following attributes (A1 - brand awareness, A2 - affordable price, A3 - photo quality, A4 - touch screen, A5 - battery health, A6 - ecosystem, A7 - water proof, A8 - interface, A9 - system performance, A10 - video recording), all the team members need to assess the seven product series based on the above-mentioned attributes using a five-point Likert scale. Then, calculating the average assessment and input for MDS. Draw a 2D plot to show product positioning and address managerial insights in a short report. (You may not use all the product series. In this case, please read product review reports to find experts' comments.)

繪製 2D 定位圖呈現產品定位，並撰寫短報告提出管理意涵。
